## My Favorite Movie

Interstellar is a movie about a pilot named Cooper. He goes into space with a team to find a place where people can live. His daughter Murph stays on Earth and tries to solve a problem that could help everyone leave.

### Main Characters

- Cooper is the pilot.
- Murph is Cooper's daughter.
- Brand is one of the scientists on the mission.

### Why I Like the Movie

1. The scenes in space are fun to watch.
2. I liked how time passed differently on the planet near the black hole.
